## Links/References

### Tools

- x86-64 Assembly
    - resources on [Andrew Tolmach's page](http://web.cecs.pdx.edu/~apt/cs491/x86-64.html) and notably [these handy notes](https://usr.lmf.cnrs.fr/~jcf/ens/compil/x86-64.pdf)
    - the fascicle [x86-64 Machine-Level Programming](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~fp/courses/15213-s07/misc/asm64-handout.pdf) by Bryant and O'Hallaron
    - the [`gdb`](https://www.gnu.org/software/gdb/) debugger can be used with x86-64 assembly code (step by step execution, register display, etc.); see [this tutorial](https://beej.us/guide/bggdb/)
    - [Compiler Explorer](https://godbolt.org/#) is the best way to inspect the code generated by compilers 

- To play with other architectures
    - MIPS : [MARS](https://dpetersanderson.github.io/) and [SPIM](http://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~larus/spim.html)([doc](https://usr.lmf.cnrs.fr/~jcf/ens/compil/HP_AppA.pdf)) simulators
    - RISC-V : [Arrakis](https://codeberg.org/Arrakis/arrakis) ([doc](https://codeberg.org/Arrakis/arrakis/wiki)) simulator/debugger

- OCaml
    - plenty of resources at [ocaml.org](https://ocaml.org/)
    - a good book : [Learn Programming with OCaml](https://usr.lmf.cnrs.fr/lpo/)
    - [`ocamllex` documentation](https://caml.inria.fr/pub/docs/manual-ocaml/lexyacc.html)
    - [Menhir](https://gallium.inria.fr/~fpottier/menhir/), a LR(1) parser generator for OCaml
    - [Cairn](https://github.com/VincentPenelle/cairn), a tool for visualize LR parser produced by Menhir 



### Books/Papers related to the Course
- Alfred V. Aho, Monica S. Lam, Ravi Sethi et Jeffrey D. Ullman, [*Modern Compilers: Principles, Techniques, and Tools*](https://suif.stanford.edu/dragonbook/) (the Dragon Book)
- Benjamin C. Pierce, [*Types and Programming Languages*](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~bcpierce/tapl/index.html)
- Randal E. Bryant et David R. O'Hallaron, [*Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective*](https://csapp.cs.cmu.edu/)
